# Seasonal grounds maintenance

Reference: DEMO-001 / Fictional procurement enquiry

## Scope of work

Maintain the landscaped grounds of a community facility during the growing season.
The reviewer needs a supplier comparison with traceable service information and a
separate list of documents to request before qualification.

## Technical requirements

- Mowing
- Hedge trimming
- Seasonal cleanup

## Mandatory requirements

- Certificate of liability insurance
- Two recent references for comparable work

## Place of performance

Ontario, Canada. Confirm service coverage directly with each supplier.
